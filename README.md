# Ifakara Connect cards

The published site for the Ifakara Connect initiative at Ifakara Health Institute:
staff scan a QR code, enter their IHI email and see their own match card.

Live at https://map-data-engineering.github.io/ihi-connect-cards/

Each card is encrypted with its owner's email; this repo holds no readable staff data.
Everything here is generated by `card_site.py` in the private `ihi-connect` repo. Don't edit these files by hand:
rebuild there and copy the contents of `docs/` here.
